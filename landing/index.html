What one line looks like

너와 같이 걷고 싶어

neo-wa ga-chi geot-kko si-peo

I want to walk with you

같이 → ga-chi The ㅌ softens into “ch” before 이. You won’t hear “gat-i”.

걷고 → geot-kko The ㄱ turns tense after ㄷ. Textbook spelling misses this.
