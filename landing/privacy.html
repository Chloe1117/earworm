Earworm waitlist

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

The short version

We collect only what you type into the waitlist form. We use your email for one message: telling you when Earworm launches. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use ads or tracking cookies on this site.

What we collect

DataWhy
EmailTo send you one launch email
CountryTo decide which countries to launch in first
Favorite group, first songOptional. To choose the first practice routines we build
Price answerTo decide a fair price
Link source, browser languageTo see which posts brought you here
Sign-up timeRecord keeping

We look at country, price and song answers only as totals, not person by person.

Where it’s stored

Your answers are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, which acts as our data processor. The waitlist can’t be read from this website; only the Earworm team can view it.

How long we keep it

We keep your answers until 30 days after launch, then delete your email. If Earworm doesn’t launch, we delete the whole list within 12 months of the date you signed up.

Age

The waitlist is for people aged 13 or older (16 or older in the EU). If you’re younger, please don’t sign up. If we learn that someone under that age signed up, we delete their entry.

Your choices

  • Leave the waitlist or delete your data: email us and we’ll delete it within 30 days.
  • See what we have: ask and we’ll send you a copy.
  • Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe.
